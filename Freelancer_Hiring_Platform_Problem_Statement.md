Problem Statement

1.  Title

Freelancer Hiring Platform (Upwork-Style)

2.  Domain

Job Marketplace / Freelancing / Recruitment Platform

3.  Users (3 Roles)

Admin: Manages users, verifies freelancers, monitors projects, resolves
disputes, and generates reports. Client: Posts projects, reviews
proposals, hires freelancers, tracks progress, releases payments, and
provides ratings. Freelancer: Creates profile, searches projects,
submits proposals, completes work, receives payments, and reviews
clients.

4.  Problem

Many businesses struggle to find skilled freelancers, while freelancers
face difficulty finding genuine projects. Traditional hiring lacks
transparency and centralized project, proposal, payment, and review
management.

5.  Proposed Solution

Develop a web-based Freelancer Hiring Platform where clients post
projects, freelancers submit proposals, clients hire suitable
freelancers, track progress, manage milestone payments, and exchange
ratings and reviews.

6.  Core Entities

User

Freelancer Profile

Project

Proposal

Contract

Payment

Review

7.  Roles & Permissions

Admin: Manage users/projects, verify freelancers, resolve disputes,
manage settings. Client: Manage projects, hire freelancers, release
payments, submit reviews. Freelancer: Manage profile, submit proposals,
update project progress, receive payments.

8.  Success Criteria

A client should be able to post a project in under 5 minutes.

A freelancer should be able to submit a proposal in under 2 minutes.

Clients should be able to hire a freelancer within 10 minutes.

Real-time project status updates and notifications.

Secure tracking of projects and payments.

9.  Out of Scope

No real-time video/audio calling.

No cryptocurrency or international payment gateway.

No AI-based recommendation.

Web application only.

No multi-tenant support.

No external job portal integration.
